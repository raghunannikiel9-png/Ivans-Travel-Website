Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

How many passengers can the V-Class carry?

The service is set up for a maximum of 6 passengers.

Yes. King Shaka International Airport transfers are available, including flight details in the booking request.

Can I book a return trip?

Yes. Select “Yes” for a return trip and add the return details in the message field.

Do you operate at night?

Yes. The business operates 24/7 by arrangement.

Can I request a custom destination?

Yes. Type the destination or full address into the booking form and Ivan can quote the trip.

Are the prices fixed?

The displayed prices are starting prices. The final quote can depend on the exact pickup point, route, waiting time and requirements.
