Ivan Raghunan — Owner & Driver
About your driver

A dedicated driver, not a dispatch centre.

Ivan's Travel & Tours is run personally by Ivan Raghunan, a Durban-based chauffeur with 8 years of experience behind the wheel. There's no call centre and no rotating roster of drivers — when you book, Ivan is the one who arrives, drives, and sees you safely to where you're going.

What started as airport transfer work has grown into a full transportation service: private tours, corporate transport, and point-to-point trips across Durban, Umhlanga, Ballito and the wider KZN coast, all in a clean, comfortable vehicle.

01

One driver, every trip

You deal directly with Ivan from booking to drop-off — no handovers, no surprises.

02

Local knowledge

Years of Durban roads and routes mean smarter timing around traffic, and the best way to wherever you're headed.
